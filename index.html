Source: 2024 — AQMN_Weekly_Monthly_2024.xlsx (Table 1 weekly, Table 2 monthly averages). 2025 and 2026 — 2025.xlsx and 2026.xlsx (daily city-wise and station-wise values); weekly values are the mean of the daily city values in 7-day blocks counted from 1 January, monthly values the mean of the daily values, AQI the mean of the daily AQI, and station counts are stations reporting at least one valid day in the period. Weeks are assigned to the month in which they start. 2026 data runs to 30 October (week 44 covers only 29–30 Oct; Nov–Dec 2026 are not yet available). O₃ is not reported in the 2025 and 2026 workbooks, so it shows no data for those years. In the 2026 workbook the Delhi PM2.5 city row duplicated PM10, so it was rebuilt from the station sheet. Reference limits are the NAAQS 24-hour standards (PM2.5 60, PM10 100, SO₂ 80, NO₂ 80 µg/m³; O₃ 8-hour 100 µg/m³) — weekly and monthly means are compared against them only as an indicator, not as a formal compliance test. AQI follows the CPCB categories (Good ≤50, Satisfactory ≤100, Moderate ≤200, Poor ≤300, Very poor ≤400, Severe >400). Data caution: several localities are averaged from only 1–4 stations (shown in tooltips), and Sonipat is blank for many weeks, so its lines and cells have gaps. Map: the basemap is read first from the local tiles/{layer}/{z}/{x}/{y} folder beside this file, then from tiles saved in your browser’s IndexedDB, then from the selected online provider (Google or Esri) when online; use of those tiles is subject to the provider’s terms. Tiles that exist in none of these are blank. Locality markers are approximate. Everything else runs in your browser with no external libraries.

Source: 2024.xlsx, 2025.xlsx and 2026.xlsx (daily city-wise and station-wise values). Weekly values are the mean of the daily city values in 7-day blocks counted from 1 January (a week needs at least half its days), AQI is the mean of the daily AQI, and station counts are stations reporting at least one valid day in the period. Weeks are assigned to the month in which they start. Data runs to 30 October 2026; November–December 2026 are not yet available. In the 2026 workbook the Delhi PM2.5 city row duplicated PM10, so it was rebuilt from the station sheet. Display period follows the chart type: Full timeline and Yearly range use weekly values, Date range and Monthly range use daily values up to about three months and weekly values beyond that. Reference limits are the NAAQS 24-hour standards (PM2.5 60, PM10 100, SO₂ 80, NO₂ 80 µg/m³) — daily, weekly and monthly means are compared against them only as an indicator, not as a formal compliance test. AQI follows the CPCB categories (Good ≤50, Satisfactory ≤100, Moderate ≤200, Poor ≤300, Very poor ≤400, Severe >400). Data caution: several localities are averaged from only 1–4 stations (shown in tooltips), and Faridabad and Sonipat have long gaps in 2025 (Sonipat also in 2024), so their lines and cells have gaps. In the 2024 workbook the Delhi city values from 9 November sit about 2.5% below the mean of the 40 listed Delhi stations; the city rows are used as given. Map: the basemap is read first from the local tiles/{layer}/{z}/{x}/{y} folder beside this file, then from tiles saved in your browser’s IndexedDB, then from the selected online provider (Google or Esri) when online; use of those tiles is subject to the provider’s terms. Tiles that exist in none of these are blank. Locality markers are approximate. Everything else runs in your browser with no external libraries.
